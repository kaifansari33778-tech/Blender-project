# El Chapo - how he got caught (Documentary Template 1)

| # | Line |
|---|---|
| 1 | One actress. One secret meeting in the mountains. And the most wanted drug lord on Earth was finished. |
| 2 | Joaquín Guzmán. El Chapo. Boss of the Sinaloa Cartel. He flooded America with cocaine, and he escaped prison twice. |
| 3 | In 2014, marines cornered him in Culiacán. He slipped out through a tunnel hidden under a bathtub, and was caught days later at a beach hotel in Mazatlán. |
| 4 | Mexico locked him inside Altiplano, its most secure prison. Seventeen months later, he walked into his shower and vanished, down a mile-long tunnel with a motorcycle on rails. |
| 5 | Then he made one mistake. He became obsessed with Kate del Castillo, the actress who played a drug queen on TV. He texted her. He wanted her to make a movie about his life. |
| 6 | In October 2015, she flew into the Sinaloa mountains to meet him, and she brought Hollywood star Sean Penn. Mexican officials say that trip helped put them on his trail. |
| 7 | January 8th, 2016. Four forty in the morning. Mexican marines storm a safe house in Los Mochis. |
| 8 | His gunmen fight back with assault rifles and a rocket launcher. Five of them are killed. One marine is wounded. |
| 9 | Chapo runs again, through a secret door behind a mirror and into the city sewers. He steals a car. Marines stop him on the highway. |
| 10 | Mexico's president posts: "Mission accomplished. We have him." A year later, Chapo is flown to New York. |
| 11 | Convicted on ten counts. Life in prison plus thirty years, and twelve point six billion dollars to hand over. His wife Emma was later jailed in the U.S. too. |
| 12 | And when soldiers grabbed his son in Culiacán in 2019, cartel gunmen took over the entire city, until the army let him go. |
| 13 | The man who beat two prisons and an army was undone by a crush. Follow for more. |
